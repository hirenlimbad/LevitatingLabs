## Why an Open Research Notebook?
- ⚡ **Rapid Knowledge Sharing**: Publish empirical benchmarks, ablation studies, and negative results as soon as experiments finish.
- 🔬 **Complete Transparency**: Release unvarnished metrics, code artifacts, and datasets without waiting for publication pipelines.
- 🤝 **Immediate Peer Feedback**: Enable the research community to inspect methodologies, reproduce results, and collaborate open-source.
---

## Lab Rules & Core Principles

We adhere strictly to empirical rigor, scientific transparency, and benchmark integrity:

1. **No Shortcut Evaluation**  
   Evaluation data is strictly leakage-free: conformer generation, molecular scaffold, and train/test splits are kept physically disjoint.

2. **Standardized Baselines**  
   Every experiment is benchmarked against established baselines—ensuring honest, transparent reporting even when there is no performance gain.

3. **First-Principles Research**  
   We prioritize solving fundamental scientific bottlenecks over chasing minor hyperparameter gains on saturated benchmarks.
